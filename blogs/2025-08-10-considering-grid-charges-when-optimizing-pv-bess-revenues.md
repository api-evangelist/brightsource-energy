While wholesale electricity prices remain the primary driver of trading profitability, they are far from the only factor. In many countries - and even within specific regions - Distribution System Operators (DSOs) and Transmission System Operators (TSOs) apply variable grid fees. These charges often depend on time of day, voltage level, and day of the week.
