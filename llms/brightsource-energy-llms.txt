# BrightSource Energy

> BrightSource Energy is a renewable energy technology company founded in 2006. It pioneered utility-scale Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) — designing and building the 377 MW Ivanpah Solar Electric Generating System in California plus CSP projects in Israel, Dubai and South Africa — and in 2022 transformed into a software company. It now commercializes OASES, a cloud-based, AI-powered Energy Management System (EMS) for portfolios of photovoltaics, battery energy storage (BESS), wind, microgrids and EV charging. Engineering is in Jerusalem, Israel, with a UK office in London.

## Status of the API surface

- BrightSource Energy publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, and no machine-readable API contract (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server or A2A agent card) as of 2026-08-02.
- OASES is sold as a commercial SaaS/EMS platform. Integration is arranged directly with the company; the product page describes an "OASES Gateway + IoT" SCADA edge controller and connections to ISO/RTO and utility market interfaces, inverters/chargers and EV charging infrastructure, but publishes no protocol or endpoint documentation.
- Contact <info@brightsourceenergy.com> for platform or integration enquiries.
